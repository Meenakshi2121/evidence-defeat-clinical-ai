# Protocol amendments through v1.0

## Amendment 1: transparent development followed by prospective validation

**Date adopted:** 14 October 2026

Stage C was designated as transparent, results-informed framework development.
Its estimates are not confirmatory evidence. A separate frozen candidate,
blinded variance pilot and one-time validation were required before making the
paper's principal empirical claim.

## Amendment 2: final selection of C002

**Date adopted:** 23 October 2026

C002 replaced C001 after prespecified revision screening, fairness stress and
finalist robustness. C002 preserves C001's three essential evidence checks and
adds a graded within-fold uncertainty filter. The filter cannot compensate for
a failed essential check. The complete candidate history, including rejected
versions and adverse results, remains in the repository.

## Amendment 3: Stage D approval and registration mechanism

**Date adopted:** 23 October 2026

Stage D is authorised by the study author and registered through the public
Git tag `protocol-v1.0`. The tag, frozen configuration and complete candidate
history establish the pre-validation time stamp. This governance amendment is
made before Stage E or Stage F results are examined and does not alter C002 or
its statistical decision rule.

## Amendment 4: no weighted fairness score

**Date adopted:** 23 October 2026

Fairness remains a secondary safety-and-access audit. Raw group-specific
coverage, unsafe acceptance and utility are reported separately;
evidence-standardised comparisons are conditional on balance adequacy. No
weighted fairness composite is used because a favourable aggregate score could
hide harm or loss of access in one group.

## Post-freeze implementation record: Stage E replicate-count decision

**Date recorded:** 24 October 2026

The blinded Stage E variance pilot completed all ten scenario groups and 250
pilot replicates without exposing mean effects, method rankings or group
differences. Applying the prospectively specified rule selected 1,000 Stage F
replicates per scenario, the protocol minimum. This is an authorised
computational decision under protocol v1.0, not a change to C002, the estimand,
scenarios, thresholds, comparators, outcomes or success criterion. Stage F may
begin only from the public tag `stage-e-variance-complete-v1.0`.
